# Community-Engagement-Program

Digital Literacy For Senior Citizens

Problem statement :
<br>
Many senior citizens face difficulties in using smartphones, computers, and online services due to limited digital knowledge and lack of confidence.
As a result, they struggle to access online banking, healthcare services, digital payments, government portals, and communication platforms, 
making them dependent on others.

Objectives :
<br>
1.To improve basic digital literacy among senior citizens.<br>
2.To teach the safe use of smartphones and the internet.<br>
3.To create awareness about cyber security and online fraud.<br>
4.To help senior citizens use digital payment application.<br>
5.To help them access online healthcare services, such as booking appointments and downloading medical reports.<br>
6.To familiarize them with government websites and online public services.<br>
7.To teach them how to use communication applications such as WhatsApp, video calling, and email.<br>
8.To develop confidence and independence in using digital devices and online services.<br>
9.To teach senior citizens how to identify and avoid phishing, fake calls, suspicious links, and online scams.<br>
10.To educate them about privacy, strong passwords, OTP safety, and two-factor authentication.<br>
11.To help them understand basic social media usage and responsible digital communication.<br>
12.To teach them how to search for reliable information on the internet and identify misleading or fake information.<br>
13.To encourage senior citizens to adopt digital technology in their daily activities and services.<br>
14.To reduce their dependence on family members or others for basic digital tasks.<br>
15.To promote a sense of digital confidence, independence, and social inclusion among senior citizens.
