# Community-Engagement-Program

Digital Literacy For Senior Citizens

Problem statement :
<br>
Many senior citizens face difficulties in using smartphones, computers, and online services due to limited digital knowledge and lack of confidence.
As a result, they struggle to access online banking, healthcare services, digital payments, government portals, and communication platforms, 
making them dependent on others.

Objectives :
<br>
1.To improve basic digital literacy among senior citizens.<br>
2.To teach the safe use of smartphones and the internet.<br>
3.To create awareness about cyber security and online fraud.<br>
4.To help senior citizens use digital payment application.
